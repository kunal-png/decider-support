Frequently asked questions

How does Decider pick?

Each option gets a fair shot. Points land on random options one at a time, and the first option to reach four points wins.

How do I add, edit or delete options?

Type in “Add an option” and press Return (or tap ⊕). On iPhone, tap an option to edit it and swipe left to delete. On Mac, double-click to edit and press Delete to remove.

How do I make another list?

On iPhone, go back to your lists and tap +. On Mac, click “New list” in the sidebar or press ⌘N. Swipe, long-press or right-click a list to rename or delete it.

How do I use the widget?

Add the Decider widget to your iPhone Home Screen or Mac desktop (small or medium). Long-press it and choose “Edit Widget” to pick a list, then tap Decide right on the widget. Tapping anywhere else opens that list in the app.

Do my lists sync between iPhone and Mac?

Not yet — each device keeps its own lists. Sync is on the wish list.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Decider has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
