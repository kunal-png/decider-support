Decider does not collect any data

Decider does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • Your lists and options are saved only on your device, inside the app’s own storage (shared with the Decider widget on the same device).
  • Decider has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • Decider does not connect to the internet.

If you delete the app, your lists are deleted with it.
